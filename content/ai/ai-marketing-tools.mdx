The best AI marketing tools for a startup are the ones that cover one job each: an assistant for content, one tool for SEO, one for social, one for email, and automations for Instagram DMs. Most roundups list 20 to 35 tools and are written by the companies that sell one of them. This is the short version, grouped by job, with what each tool costs.

Prices and free limits verified November 2026. I re-check them every month.

This list is for founders and small startup teams doing marketing without a marketing hire. Every tool here is one a single person can run, and it's mostly what I actually use day to day.

## Which AI marketing tools do you actually need?

You need one tool per job, and most early-stage startups have five jobs: writing content, getting found in search, posting on social, converting Instagram comments into leads, and emailing your list. Paid ads are a sixth if you run them. Pick one tool for each and ignore the rest until something breaks.

The shift worth knowing about is from chat-based AI to agentic AI. A chatbot gives you text to paste somewhere. An agent connects to your tools through MCP (a standard plug that lets an AI app work inside other software) and does the work there. I explain the difference in [Chat-Based AI vs Agentic AI](/ai/chat-based-ai-vs-agentic-ai).

## Content and copy

**ChatGPT.** Good for drafting posts, scripts, emails, and page copy, and for quick research and second opinions. There's a free plan; check chatgpt.com/pricing for current paid plans.

**content-creation-skills.** A free, open-source pack of skills (saved instructions an AI assistant loads for a task) for hooks, scripts, captions, carousels, newsletters, and content planning. Get it on [GitHub](https://github.com/kingscrosslabs/content-creation-skills).

**aeo-seo-skills.** A free, open-source pack for SEO and AEO (getting cited by AI answers): audits, blog posts, and comparison pages. Get it on [GitHub](https://github.com/kingscrosslabs/aeo-seo-skills).

**marketingskills.** Corey Haines' open-source [marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) pack covers copywriting, CRO, and launches. Install steps for all of these are in [How to Install Free Marketing Skills for Claude](/ai/how-to-install-free-marketing-skills-claude).

**Humanizer.** An open-source skill that strips the patterns that make AI writing read like AI. See [Humanizer](/ai/humanizer).

> **Key insight:** The chatbot matters less than what you feed it. The same assistant writes generic copy with no context and usable copy with your voice, past posts, and a marketing skill loaded.

## Design and video

**Claude.** Claude, and especially Claude Code, has become a good alternative for video editing. You describe the edit, and it writes and renders the video for you with a framework like HyperFrames. Claude Pro is $20 a month billed monthly, or $17 a month billed annually, and there is a free plan.

**HyperFrames.** HeyGen's open-source framework (Apache 2.0) that turns HTML into an MP4 video. It was built for AI agents, so Claude Code can write a video like a web page and render it, with no per-render fees.

**Paper.** A design tool whose canvas is real HTML and CSS. It connects to Claude Code through MCP, so an agent can create and edit designs on the canvas, and the result exports as code. Check paper.design for current plans.

## SEO and AI search visibility

**OpenSEO.** An open-source SEO tool for keyword research and AI search visibility checks (whether ChatGPT and others mention your brand). It works through MCP, so Claude can run research directly. It runs on credits, so you pay for what you use; check its pricing page for current plans. Pair it with the free [aeo-seo-skills](https://github.com/kingscrosslabs/aeo-seo-skills) pack so Claude runs the audit and drafts the fixes. Walkthrough: [How to Audit Your Website for SEO and AEO with AI](/ai/how-to-audit-website-seo-aeo-with-ai).

## Social publishing and analytics

**Adeli.** Adeli puts social publishing, comments, DMs, analytics, and ads for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X behind one MCP. In practice, Claude drafts, schedules, and publishes my posts through it, so I skip the scheduler UI. Free is $0 but small: 10 posts, 100 comments, and 100 messages in total, not per month, and analytics needs a paid plan. Starter is $25 a month.

## Instagram comment-to-DM

This is the "comment MIKA and I'll DM you the link" trick. A tool watches your comments for a keyword and sends a DM automatically.

**LedaChat.** You set up comment-to-DM and story-reply automations by telling Claude what you want. The free plan allows 10 live automations. Pro is $25 a month and removes the limit. It's MIT licensed, so you can [self-host it](https://github.com/kingscrosslabs/ledachat) on Vercel and Neon Postgres.

## Paid ads

**Adeli.** Adeli connects Claude to your Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn ad accounts through MCP. You can create campaigns, pull results, and build your own reporting dashboards instead of clicking through each ads manager. New campaigns are created paused, and nothing spends money until you switch them on, so review before activating.

For Meta specifically, the walkthrough is [How to Connect Meta Ads to Claude](/ai/how-to-connect-meta-ads-to-claude).

## Email

**Loops.** Email marketing for newsletters and campaigns. The free plan covers 1,000 subscribed contacts and 4,000 sends per 30 days with Loops branding; Growth starts at $49 a month.

**Resend.** Transactional email, the automatic messages your product sends, like receipts, sign-in links, and welcome emails. Free covers 3,000 emails a month with a 100-a-day cap. Pro is $20 a month for 50,000 emails.

## Social listening

**Octolens.** Watches Reddit, X, Hacker News, LinkedIn, GitHub, and more for mentions of your brand, competitors, or keywords, then uses AI to filter out the posts that aren't about you and sends the rest to Slack or email. It also has an MCP, so Claude can read the mentions and draft replies. Paid plans start at $159 a month billed annually, with a 7-day free trial.

## Forms and research

**Tally.** Forms for lead capture, waitlists, surveys, and customer research. The free plan includes unlimited forms and submissions. Pro is $24 a month and removes Tally branding. It also connects to Claude through MCP, so Claude can build a form and read the responses.

## Which AI marketing tools should you skip?

**Profound, if you're small.** Enterprise pricing for a job a cheaper tool like OpenSEO does.

**Website builders like Webflow.** I used Webflow at a previous company. Building the site directly with an AI coding agent is far better, and it lets me run SEO and AEO workflows that feed growth.

**Anything that duplicates your assistant.** A standalone AI writer when you already have ChatGPT or Claude, or a second scheduler when one works. Skip tools that sell you tasks your assistant can already do.

## Other good options

- **Wispr Flow:** dictation that makes drafting faster. It's about 3x faster than my keyboard.
- **Notion:** where I keep content calendars and docs.
