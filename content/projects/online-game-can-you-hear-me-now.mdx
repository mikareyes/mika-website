I created this game, turning a weakness of Zoom into a strength.

## What is the Can You Hear Me Now? game?

Can You Hear Me Now? is a team party game for Zoom or any video call. One player goes on mute and has to get their team to guess a three-word sentence without speaking. Everyone has been stuck yelling "you're on mute" at a frozen colleague, so the game turns that moment into the fun.

![Can You Hear Me Now? online game title card](/projects/covers/online-game-can-you-hear-me-now-0.png)

## How do you play Can You Hear Me Now?

1. Split the group into two teams. Each team picks a volunteer to play the round.
2. The volunteer goes on mute. The host sends them a sentence privately in the Zoom chat. Every sentence is exactly 3 words long. No more, no less.
3. The volunteer has 2 minutes to get their team to guess as many sentences as possible.
4. A sentence only counts when at least one teammate says the complete sentence out loud.
5. This is not charades. The muted player can nod yes or no, make facial expressions, and hold up fingers to show the number of words or syllables. They cannot act out the words themselves.
6. Play 4 rounds, 2 volunteers per team. The team with the most sentences guessed wins.

![Can You Hear Me Now? game instructions](/projects/covers/online-game-can-you-hear-me-now-1.png)

## Where do you get three-word sentences for the game?

I pull prompts from this list of three-word quotes: [170 best three-word quotes](https://medium.com/funny-quotes/170-best-three-word-quotes-motivational-attitude-love-e0f01fd44d43). Short, famous phrases work best because the team can lock onto one word and guess the rest.

## What do you need to play?

A video call with chat (Zoom works), at least two people per team, and a host to send sentences and keep time. It works for remote team building, virtual happy hours, and family calls.
